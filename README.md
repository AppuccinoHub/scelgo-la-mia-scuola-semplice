## What it is

A simple Italian 2 project for everyone. Students pick an Italian high school in Rome, warm up with key Italian words, write a 5-sentence script, and record 3 slides (about one minute).

- Start screen: says **This is a project.**, gives a 4-sentence summary with one Listen button, shows the 5 steps, and has one big orange button, **Start here: How Italian high schools work**. **Word warm-up**, **Schools** and **Class view** are smaller buttons.
- 5 steps, one screen each: Learn how schools work, Pick a type of school then a real school in Rome, Warm up with the words, Write your script, Record your slides. Every screen has a short "Tap here / Now tap Next" line, and the Next button glows when students can go on.
- **Word warm-up** (step 3): 20 Italian words (sono, è, ho, anni, frequento, vorrei, scelgo, perché, ma, invece, anche, mi piace...), each with a Listen button, then three short rounds: hear the word and tap the English; type the Italian with accent buttons; fill the right word into a script line. No timer and no score. A wrong answer explains why. Progress is saved on the device.
- Step 2 has two parts: **2a** pick a type of school (6 cards, or **None of these? See all school types** for all 17), then **2b** pick a real school in Rome from that type, on a simple drawn map of Rome (Tevere, major landmarks, and a pin for each school).
- The script can use **ma**, **invece** or **anche** to compare the two schools.
- Sound is recorded Italian stored inside the file (no computer voices, no internet): the Italian words and phrases students learn or say (warm-up words, the school-type names on the 6 cards and 17 types, the script lines), plus one short English clip for the start summary and one for each step's main instruction.
- **Class view** (button at the top, or `?class=1`): bigger text and Listen buttons for a projector. Sound plays only when you tap a Listen button.
- **Teacher view** (`?teacher=1`): 3 simple check questions under each school card, answers on tap. Students never see them.
- Tap any yellow Italian word, or any abbreviation in a school name (IIS, ITIS, IPSEOA...), for a small Learn more panel that works offline, with a "Learn more at home" link to an official site.
- Works from one file with no internet. No names are asked or stored.
