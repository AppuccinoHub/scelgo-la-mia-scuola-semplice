## What it is

A simple Italian 2 project for everyone. Students pick an Italian high school in Rome, write a 5-sentence script, and record 3 slides (about one minute).

- Start screen: says **This is a project.**, gives a 4-sentence summary with a Listen button, shows the 4 steps, and has one big orange button, **Start here: How Italian high schools work**. **Schools** and **Class view** are smaller buttons.
- 4 steps, one screen each: Learn how schools work, Pick a school type, Write your script, Record your slides. Every screen has a short "Tap here / Now tap Next" line, and the Next button glows when students can go on.
- Step 2 shows 6 school cards. A student who wants none of them taps **None of these? See all school types** to pick from all 17 types, each with a real Rome school card. The pick flows into the script and slides the same way.
- Listen buttons play recorded Italian and English audio stored inside the file (no computer voices needed, works offline). Every one of the 6 cards and all 17 school types has its own Listen button.
- **Class view** (button at the top, or `?class=1`): bigger text and Listen buttons for a projector. Sound plays only when you tap a Listen button, at full volume.
- **Teacher view** (`?teacher=1`): 3 simple check questions under each school card, answers on tap. Students never see them. Printable versions are on the downloads page.
- Tap any yellow Italian word, or any abbreviation in a school name (IIS, ITIS, IPSEOA...), for a small Learn more panel that works offline, with a "Learn more at home" link to an official site.
- Works from one file with no internet. No names are asked or stored.
